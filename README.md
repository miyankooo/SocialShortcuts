# Social Shortcuts

A WoW Forever addon that lets you whisper, invite, ask to join or befriend a player by holding a modifier key and left-clicking their name. It skips the right-click menu for the social actions you use most.

## Features

- Whisper, Add Friend and Invite, each on its own modifier key.
- Works on names in chat, unit frames, the friends list, the Who list, the guild roster and Group Finder.
- Battle.net friends get a Battle.net whisper, and Invite reaches the character they play.
- Inviting into a full party offers to convert it to a raid.
- Invite works like the game's own menu entry: it invites when you can, suggests the player to your leader when you are in a group without lead, and asks to join when you are alone and they are in a group. Turn off asking to join with Invite Asks to Join in the settings.

## Usage

1. Hold the key for an action and left-click a player name.
2. To change the keys, type `/ssc` or pick Social Shortcuts in the addon menu.
3. Pick a key for each action, or None to turn it off. Defaults restores the starting keys.

Starting keys:

- macOS: Option whispers, Control adds a friend, Command invites.
- Windows: Ctrl whispers, Alt invites. Add Friend starts on None, so give it a key if you want it.

The shortcuts are off in dungeons, raids and PvP matches, where the game hides player names.

In chat, a plain click on a name opens a whisper as usual, and a shortcut click runs only the shortcut. Shift stays the game's own: Shift-click on a name inserts it or runs a /who.
